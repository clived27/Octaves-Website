About Us

A Legacy of
Sound & Soul

Established in 2012, the Octaves Music Club is a dynamic community of music enthusiasts at VNIT, united by a shared passion for creating and experiencing music. The club provides a platform for students to explore their musical talents and express themselves through diverse genres and styles.

Octaves has built a strong reputation through stellar performances year-round at both student-led and official events. Students from Octaves have proudly represented VNIT at numerous inter-collegiate competitions like Mood Indigo IIT Bombay, one of Asia's largest cultural festivals.
