About Us

A Legacy of
Sound & Soul

Founded within the walls of Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur, Octaves has grown into one of the most celebrated music clubs in the institute's history. From classical ragas to contemporary compositions, we celebrate every genre.

Our members perform at inter-collegiate festivals, national competitions, and intimate campus concerts that have become cherished traditions across academic years.
