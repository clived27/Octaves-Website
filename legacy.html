About Us

Where Music
Finds a Home

Founded within the walls of Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology, Nagpur, Octaves has grown into one of the most celebrated music clubs in the institute's history. From classical ragas to contemporary compositions, we celebrate every genre.

Our members perform at inter-collegiate festivals, national competitions, and intimate campus concerts that have become cherished traditions across academic years.

Our Journey

Milestones in Music

Est.

The First Note

Octaves was founded by a small group of music enthusiasts at VNIT who believed the campus deserved a stage for every voice and every instrument.

Aarohi

The Annual Showcase

Aarohi became the flagship annual concert — a night where the campus holds its breath and Octaves delivers something unforgettable every single year.

Fests

Inter-College Stages

Octaves began representing VNIT at inter-collegiate music festivals across the country, bringing back medals and memories in equal measure.

Today

Still Playing

With a 2025–26 council of 8 talented musicians, Octaves continues to grow — louder, bolder, and more musically diverse than ever.
